Delete your StepForge account

Secure account and associated-data deletion

Sign in with the Google account connected to StepForge. Your email address is not sent to StepForge support and you do not need to send a deletion request manually.

1. Verify your StepForge account

Google will let you choose an account. StepForge deletes only the Firebase account authenticated by Google — the page cannot delete another user by entering an email address.

VERIFIED

2. Permanently delete the account

After verification, you can permanently delete the selected StepForge account. This action cannot be undone.

  • Your StepForge Firebase Authentication account is deleted.
  • Your StepForge cloud profile, backups and Premium entitlement record are deleted.
  • StepForge billing-account associations and purchase-token records linked to this StepForge account are deleted.
  • If a renewable Google Play StepForge subscription is linked, future renewals are stopped before deletion. This does not automatically refund previous charges.
This does not delete your Google Account. It also cannot remotely erase StepForge data that exists only on one of your devices. Local-only data can be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling StepForge.

Privacy and security

StepForge never asks you to type your Google password into this page. Google handles the sign-in window. The deletion backend identifies the account from Firebase Authentication and does not accept an email address or UID from the page as the deletion target.

Need help?

For technical problems with the deletion page, contact stepforge0@gmail.com. Email is for support only; account deletion itself is completed through the secure flow above.
