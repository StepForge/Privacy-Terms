Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how StepForge (“StepForge”, “we”, “us”, “our”, or “the App”) processes information when you install or use the App. StepForge is developed and maintained by Iman Mirzazadegan (“Developer”).

StepForge is a wellness and activity application with a local-first design. Depending on the features you use, the App may also connect to Google Health Connect, Google Sign-In, Firebase services, Google Play Billing, Google Mobile Ads/AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP).

Key privacy promise.

StepForge does not sell your personal data. Health, fitness and wellness data — including Health Connect records, steps, sleep, hydration, workouts, weight and goals — is not transferred to advertising platforms for ad targeting and is not used to personalize advertisements.

1. Scope

This Policy applies to StepForge features including step tracking, dashboards, Progress and History, achievements, streaks, goals, widgets, notifications, hydration, sleep and naps, workouts, profile personalization, App Lock, Health Connect, Google account connection, Sync & Backup, Premium, one-time purchases such as Streak Recovery, advertising on the free tier, support/debug exports and account deletion.

2. Responsible party

StepForge is developed and maintained by Iman Mirzazadegan. Privacy requests and questions can be sent to stepforge0@gmail.com.

3. Privacy at a glance

Local-first wellness data

Most activity, sleep, hydration, goals, achievements and settings are stored on your device by default.

Optional cloud and integrations

Google account, Firebase backup/restore and Health Connect are feature-dependent and controlled by you.

Ads on the free tier

Free users may see banner and app-open ads. Active Premium users are intended to have an ad-free StepForge experience.

Health data is not ad-targeting data

StepForge does not send your health/fitness data to AdMob for personalized or interest-based advertising.

5. Advertising and consent

StepForge’s free tier may display banner ads and app-open ads using Google Mobile Ads/AdMob. Active Premium users are intended not to receive StepForge advertising while their entitlement is valid.

Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect and share information such as your IP address, app/product interactions (for example app launches, ad taps or video views), diagnostic/performance information and device/account identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. The Android advertising ID can be controlled or reset through Android settings, and availability depends on device/platform settings.

For users in the EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland, StepForge uses Google’s consent tooling / User Messaging Platform where required. The consent message may allow you to consent, decline, or manage options. Depending on your choice, region, law and Google’s available ad-serving modes, StepForge may request personalized, non-personalized or limited ads.

The list of advertising technology providers can change. Where required, the current providers and purposes are shown inside Google’s consent message rather than being permanently hard-coded into this Policy.

Where the App indicates that privacy options are available, you can reopen the advertising privacy choices from StepForge settings and change eligible consent choices.

6. Premium and purchases

StepForge may offer an auto-renewing Premium subscription and one-time purchases through Google Play Billing. A current example of a one-time product is Streak Recovery. Product names, available plans, prices, currencies, taxes and offers are shown by Google Play at checkout and may vary by country.

To verify purchases and restore access, StepForge may send Google Play purchase information to the StepForge backend, including product identifiers, purchase tokens/verification references and entitlement state. This is used only for purchase validation, restoring entitlements, fraud prevention, support, and account/purchase lifecycle handling.

Google Play — not StepForge — processes your payment method and holds the underlying Google Play transaction record. Premium entitlement status may be cached locally and stored in StepForge cloud records so that paid access can be restored and synchronized.

Account deletion and purchases.

Deleting a StepForge account removes StepForge cloud profile/backup and account-linked entitlement/purchase-association records handled by StepForge. It does not erase Google Play’s own transaction history and does not automatically refund past charges. If a renewable StepForge subscription is linked, the deletion flow may request that future renewal be stopped; users should verify subscription status in Google Play.

7. Health data and advertising separation

StepForge does not use Health Connect or wellness data for ads.

Data accessed through Health Connect and other health/fitness permissions is used only for user-facing health, fitness and wellness functionality. StepForge does not transfer, sell or use that data for serving ads, personalized advertising, interest-based advertising, creditworthiness, lending, employment or insurance decisions.

This separation also applies to StepForge-native wellness data such as steps, sleep, hydration, workouts, weight, activity goals and similar health/fitness information: it is not intentionally passed to AdMob as custom targeting data.

8. Analytics and diagnostics

StepForge may use Firebase Analytics where configured to understand aggregate app usage and improve product behavior, and Firebase Crashlytics to identify crashes and stability problems. These services can process app events and technical information such as app version, device/OS information, crash state and performance diagnostics.

Advertising consent signals may also be interpreted by Google products when consent mode is enabled in the applicable Google configuration. Exact collection can depend on your region, consent choices, platform settings and the enabled StepForge build configuration.

Debug logs or exports are not automatically published. If you manually share an export by email or another app, the destination service processes that file under its own terms.

9. Data sources

  • You: data you enter, edit, purchase, connect or choose to share.
  • Your device: sensors, Android permissions, app-private storage, notifications and device settings.
  • Health Connect: approved records you explicitly allow StepForge to read/write.
  • Google account and Google Play: sign-in/profile information, purchase status and billing verification data where used.
  • Firebase/Google services: authentication, cloud data, server functions, App Check/integrity signals, analytics and crash diagnostics where configured.
  • AdMob/UMP: advertising, consent and measurement-related information described above.

10. Why we process data

  • Provide step tracking, history, goals, sleep, hydration, workouts, widgets and achievements.
  • Personalize units, profile information, estimates and goals.
  • Provide Health Connect import/export and backup/restore when enabled.
  • Authenticate users and manage cloud accounts.
  • Verify Premium and one-time purchases and restore paid access.
  • Display ads to eligible free users and measure/fraud-protect ad delivery.
  • Process consent/privacy choices and comply with legal/platform obligations.
  • Detect crashes, abuse, invalid purchase activity and reliability issues.
  • Handle support requests and account deletion.

11. Legal bases where required

Where GDPR or similar law requires a legal basis, processing may rely on: performance of the service/contract (core app and paid features), consent (for example certain Health Connect access, optional integrations and advertising/analytics storage where required), legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, reliability and limited product analytics where permitted), and legal obligations. Consent can be withdrawn for optional processing without affecting processing already lawfully performed.

12. Storage locations

12.1 On device

Most wellness data is stored in Android app-private databases, preferences, files and caches. Clearing app data or uninstalling can remove local-only data.

12.2 Health Connect

Health Connect data is managed by Android/Health Connect and remains subject to the permissions you grant there.

12.3 Firebase/cloud

When account, backup, purchase verification or cloud features are used, supported information may be processed in Firebase Authentication, Firestore/other Firebase storage, Cloud Functions and related Google infrastructure.

12.4 Advertising and billing systems

Ad-related data is processed by Google Mobile Ads/AdMob and approved ad technology providers; purchase transaction/payment information is processed by Google Play. StepForge receives only the billing information needed to verify and manage entitlement.

13. Service providers and third parties

Depending on enabled features, StepForge can use:

  • Google Health Connect — user-authorized health/fitness data access.
  • Google Sign-In / Firebase Authentication — optional account authentication.
  • Firebase Firestore / Cloud Functions — supported cloud data, backup, purchase verification and account operations.
  • Firebase App Check / Google Play Integrity — abuse protection and backend integrity checks.
  • Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics — analytics and stability diagnostics where configured.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and UMP — advertising, consent, measurement and ad-fraud prevention.
  • Google Play Billing / Developer services — Premium subscriptions and one-time purchase processing/verification.
  • Email/share destinations — only when you choose to export or share content.

Google and other external services apply their own privacy notices and terms. See Google Privacy Policy.

14. Permissions

StepForge may request Activity Recognition, Notifications, Exact Alarms, Health Connect data-type permissions, Internet/network access, and device biometric authentication through Android. Permissions depend on your Android version and the features you enable. You can grant or revoke them in Android, Health Connect and StepForge settings.

15. Health Connect

Health Connect integration is optional. Depending on the current build and permissions you approve, StepForge may read/write supported data such as steps, distance, calories, weight, sleep sessions/stages and other declared health/fitness categories needed for visible StepForge features. StepForge uses reconciliation/source information to reduce duplicates where possible.

Health Connect information can originate from other apps or devices, so it can differ from StepForge sensor data or other health apps due to sensor algorithms, rounding and synchronization timing.

16. Sync and Backup

Sync & Backup is optional and intended to restore supported StepForge state after reinstall or device change. Supported categories can include activity history, goals, settings, achievements/progress metadata, hydration, sleep and workout summaries. Security secrets, biometric data and temporary caches may be excluded. Availability and completeness depend on app version, network and backend availability.

17. Account and profile

Core local tracking may work without a StepForge cloud account. When Google Sign-In is used, StepForge may access basic information such as display name, email and profile photo URL for account identification and optional profile import. You may edit or decline profile fields where the App provides that choice.

18. Automated insights and calculations

StepForge may automatically calculate trends, estimates, achievements, streaks, goal progress, sleep summaries, activity scores and coaching-style messages from your data. These are wellness-oriented app calculations, may be imperfect, and are not medical diagnoses or professional advice.

19. Retention and deletion

  • Local data: generally remains until deleted in-app, app storage is cleared, or the App is uninstalled.
  • Cloud account and backup: retained while the feature/account is active or until deletion/overwrite according to StepForge controls.
  • Health Connect: controlled through Health Connect permissions/data management.
  • Advertising/consent data: retained by Google/ad providers according to their settings, consent status and legal requirements.
  • Purchase data: StepForge account-linked entitlement/purchase references are retained as necessary to provide and verify paid access; Google Play retains its own transaction records under Google’s policies.
  • Diagnostics: retained under the applicable Firebase/Google service configuration.
  • Legal/anti-fraud records: limited information may be retained where necessary for law, tax/accounting, fraud, security, refunds or chargeback disputes.

For secure cloud-account deletion, use Delete Account. The deletion page explains what StepForge deletes and what remains with Google Play or only on local devices.

20. Your rights and controls

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, object to processing, obtain portability, withdraw consent, and lodge a complaint with a competent data-protection authority.

  • Change Android and Health Connect permissions.
  • Manage ad/privacy choices where the App exposes them.
  • Reset/delete the Android advertising ID through device controls where available.
  • Manage or cancel Google Play subscriptions in Google Play.
  • Edit local profile/goals and disable optional integrations.
  • Delete local data or uninstall the App.
  • Delete your StepForge cloud account through the deletion page.
  • Contact us for a privacy request.

21. Security

StepForge uses Android app sandboxing, app-private storage, encrypted network transport provided by supported services, optional App Lock, Firebase authentication, backend authorization and integrity/App Check mechanisms where configured. No system is perfectly secure; users should keep Android and StepForge updated and protect their device account/lock credentials.

22. Children

StepForge is not specifically directed to children under 13. If applicable law requires parental/guardian consent for a younger user, that requirement applies. Parents or guardians may contact us regarding a child’s StepForge cloud data.

23. International processing

Google/Firebase, Google Play, AdMob and other service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their international-transfer mechanisms and safeguards are governed by their policies and applicable law.

24. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when features, service providers, advertising, purchases, platform requirements or laws change. The current version and effective date are published here. Material changes may also be communicated in the App where appropriate.
